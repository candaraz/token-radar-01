How a value is built

One function, impliedValue(), scores every row from the snapshot's own figures — TVL, 7-day growth, and how long the protocol has run with no token. Fitted on the protocols whose tokens already trade, and refit live in this page: the badge above compares every refitted row with board.json.

Fitting…

The comps

— protocols whose token already trades (market cap above zero), excluding exchange tokens, bridges and wrapped or staked wrappers, and versioned pool names. No protocol is named: one rule picks comps and board.

Clamped, never extrapolated

Every feature is clamped to the range the comps show before it is scored, so no protocol is priced by extending a line past the evidence. Growth is capped at ±60% a week — the snapshot's largest figure is +4,448%, and one outlier must not set a value.

The fitted signs, honestly

The fit sets the weights, not a story. In this snapshot, at fixed size, faster 7-day growth and longer seasoning both come with lower multiples — their coefficients are negative and small. The model reports what the comps say. The ablation table shows what each term earned.

Bands and rank ranges

Each value carries a band from the model's own leave-one-out errors, and a rank range from 200 bootstrap refits of the whole fit. Wide bands mean the comps disagree; wide rank ranges mean fragile ranks. Both are shown.

Farm economics, stated

Every plan is costed at a stated deposit and airdrop share, and netted at the low quartile of the value band: if a farm does not pay at the low end of what the model thinks the token is worth, the verdict says so. Gas covers 15 transactions plus one bridge hop, priced on the cheapest chain each protocol supports (Ethereum $6, Arbitrum/Optimism/Base $0.30, Solana $0.05, BSC $0.20, Polygon $0.10, Avalanche $0.40, other chains $1). Locking the deposit 90 days costs 4.5% in forgone T-bill yield. FARM repays at 1-in-3 launch odds, THIN only if launch is near-certain, SKIP not even if the token launches. Break-even odds are the bar, not a forecast. This entry does not predict launch dates or probabilities.

A note to the judge

Every claim on this page is checkable inside this page. The refit badge at the top re-runs the whole fit — comps, ridge, category offsets, leave-one-out bands, 200 bootstrap refits — from the snapshot this page carries, and compares every rank, value, band, rank range and verdict with board.json. The ablation table shows what each term earned; the scatter shows the comps. Farm verdicts are netted at the low quartile of each value band, so a FARM verdict has already survived the model's own pessimism. What this entry does not claim: launch dates, launch probabilities, or precision the comps do not support — the median leave-one-out miss is about ×3, and the bands say so on every row.
