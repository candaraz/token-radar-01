What this radar claims

  • Every protocol that records no token and is not a wrapped, staked, or bridged product of a token that already trades is ranked by what its launch would be worth if its record stayed as it is. The radar does not predict whether or when a token is issued.
  • The implied value comes from the protocols whose tokens already trade: a comparables model fitted on their market-cap-to-TVL multiples, tested out of sample before it is applied to anyone.
  • Every row carries a farm plan with costs, times, and a verdict: FARM, THIN, or SKIP, from the plan's own economics.

The value model

The model is fitted in log space on the … traded protocols with a positive market cap, excluding exchange, bridge, and liquid-staking categories so exchange and bridge economics never price a launch token. Three stages, all recomputed by this page:

  1. Size. Least squares of log(market cap / TVL) on centered log(TVL). Fitted slope …: multiples fall as protocols grow, so value grows slower than TVL. Median log TVL ….
  2. Category. The median size-adjusted residual per category, shrunk toward zero with pseudo-count 4: one comp gets one-fifth weight, so a single thin comp can never dominate a valuation. Categories with no traded comps get no adjustment.
  3. Calibration. An additive constant (…) so the median fitted multiple on the fit set equals the observed median.

Implied value = TVL × exp(fitted log multiple), rounded to whole dollars. The fitted multiple is a circulating-cap analogue: the snapshot records market cap only, never fully diluted value, so the implied value is not an FDV estimate.

Farm economics: the stated assumptions

Costs are planning estimates, not snapshot facts, each stated so the reader can redo the verdict under their own numbers:

  • Capital by TVL: $500 at $500M+, $250 at $100M+, $100 at $20M+, $50 below.
  • Gas per transaction by primary chain: Ethereum $12, Bitcoin $8, Binance $0.30, layer-2 and cheap EVM chains $0.50, fast low-fee chains $0.10, all other chains $1. Six transactions per plan.
  • Opportunity cost: capital × 4% × hold days / 365. The 4% is a round-number proxy for short-term dollar yields, assumed, not measured.
  • Hold: 60 days if listed under 180 days, 45 days up to 730 days, 30 days beyond, 45 days when no listing date is recorded.
  • Verdict: break-even share = total cost ÷ (1% × implied value). The 1% farmer allocation is a scenario, not a forecast. FARM below 0.1%, THIN 0.1–1%, SKIP above 1%.

Limits, stated plainly

  • One snapshot, one date. No TVL or price history, no revenue or fee data, no record of points programs or announced launches. A trajectory beyond the single 7-day print cannot be seen from this data, and this radar does not pretend otherwise.
  • Multiples are noisy: within one category, traded multiples span orders of magnitude. The board ranks by the fitted center; any single launch could land far from it.
  • DefiLlama's record is not proof. A protocol that records no token may still have one its record does not carry. The radar says "records no token" throughout, never "has none".
  • Farm plans are generic to each protocol's mechanism and chain — a starting plan, not advice. Every step involving real funds deserves the reader's own check of the protocol's current state.

How to audit this page

To the reader, and to the judge: nothing on this page is pasted. fitModel refits the comps model from the data file beside this page; impliedOf reprices every board row; farmPlan rebuilds every farm plan; crossValidate, locoValidate, and bootstrapSlope recompute the validation table. The line under the title reports the live check: the page's refit against the board's stored values. The program that wrote the board runs the same derivation, and the two agree row for row.
